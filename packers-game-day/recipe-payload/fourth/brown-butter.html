Brown Butter Chocolate-Chip Skillet Cookie + Frozen CustardOpen Recipe ↓
Serves4–6
Total Time45 minutes
DifficultyModerate
Equipment10-inch cast-iron skillet; saucepan.
Make-ahead: Make dough up to 2 days ahead. Brown the butter earlier if desired.

Ingredients

  • 8 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
  • Vanilla frozen custard
  • Hot fudge
  • Caramel sauce
  • Flaky salt, optional

Step-by-step

  1. Heat oven to 350°F.
  2. Melt butter in a light-colored saucepan over medium heat. Continue cooking, swirling often, until the milk solids turn amber and smell nutty, 4–6 minutes. Cool 10 minutes.
  3. Whisk brown butter with both sugars. Whisk in egg and vanilla.
  4. Stir in flour, baking soda and salt just until combined, then fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Press dough evenly into a lightly greased 10-inch cast-iron skillet.
  6. Bake 18–24 minutes. The edges should be browned, but the center should still look slightly soft.
  7. Cool 8–10 minutes so the center sets without becoming dry.
  8. Top warm wedges with vanilla frozen custard, hot fudge and caramel.
KaySo note: For the best texture, pull it earlier than feels natural. A skillet cookie continues cooking from residual heat.
