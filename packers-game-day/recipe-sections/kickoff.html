Wisconsin Supper Club ChickenOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3 generous servings
Total Time55 minutes
DifficultyModerate
EquipmentLarge oven-safe skillet; instant-read thermometer.
Make-ahead: Cook the bacon and slice the mushrooms earlier. The sauce is best made right before serving.

Ingredients

  • 3 boneless skinless chicken breasts, 6–8 oz each
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 3/4 tsp coarse black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 4 slices bacon, chopped
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small shallot or 1/3 small onion, minced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/3 cup brandy or 1/3 cup chicken broth
  • 3/4 cup chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 slices Swiss cheese
  • 1 tsp coarsely cracked black pepper, plus more to taste

Step-by-step

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Pound the thickest part of each chicken breast lightly so the pieces cook evenly. Season with salt, pepper and garlic powder.
  2. In an oven-safe skillet over medium heat, cook bacon until crisp. Transfer bacon to a plate, leaving about 1 tablespoon fat in the skillet.
  3. Raise heat to medium-high. Sear chicken 3–4 minutes per side until browned. Transfer temporarily to a plate.
  4. Add mushrooms and cook 5–7 minutes until browned. Add shallot and butter; cook 1 minute.
  5. Carefully add brandy and simmer 1–2 minutes. Add broth, cream, Dijon, Worcestershire and cracked pepper. Simmer 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened.
  6. Return chicken to the skillet. Spoon a little sauce over each piece, top with Swiss and scatter bacon around the pan.
  7. Transfer to the oven for 8–12 minutes, until chicken reaches 160–165°F and the cheese is melted.
  8. Rest 5 minutes. Taste the sauce for salt and spoon plenty of mushrooms and peppercorn cream over each serving.
KaySo note: If you dislike mushrooms, omit them and add an extra 1/2 sliced onion cooked until golden. The sauce should be peppery and savory, not bland cream.
Smoked Pork Tenderloin RouladeOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3 generous servings
Total Time1 hour 25 minutes
DifficultyModerate–Advanced
EquipmentSmoker; sharp knife; meat mallet or rolling pin; butcher’s twine; thermometer.
Make-ahead: Caramelize the onions and cook the bacon a day ahead. Roll and tie the pork up to 8 hours ahead.

Ingredients

  • 1 pork tenderloin, 1 1/4–1 1/2 lb
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 3/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1 medium yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1 tsp chopped fresh thyme or 1/2 tsp dried
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp melted butter for finishing

Step-by-step

  1. Set smoker to 250°F. Cook the sliced onion in butter over medium-low heat with a pinch of salt and brown sugar for 18–25 minutes, until deeply golden. Cool.
  2. Trim the tenderloin. Slice lengthwise almost all the way through and open it like a book. Make additional shallow horizontal cuts into the thicker sections so you can unfold it into a roughly even rectangle.
  3. Cover with plastic wrap and gently pound to about 1/2-inch thickness.
  4. Season the inside lightly with salt and pepper. Spread Dijon over the pork, then distribute caramelized onion, bacon, cheddar and thyme, leaving a 1-inch border.
  5. Roll tightly from a long side into a cylinder. Tie with butcher's twine every 1 1/2–2 inches.
  6. Rub the outside with olive oil, remaining salt, pepper and paprika.
  7. Smoke until the center reaches 135–138°F, usually 45–65 minutes depending on thickness.
  8. For better color, sear the roulade quickly in a screaming-hot skillet or on a hot grill, turning to brown all sides. Pull at 140–142°F.
  9. Brush with melted butter and rest 10 minutes. Remove twine and slice into 3/4-inch spirals.
KaySo note: Cheese may leak a little; that is normal. Keeping a clean border and rolling firmly minimizes it.
Wisconsin Tavern Meatloaf with Beer-Onion GravyOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3–4 servings
Total Time1 hour 35 minutes
DifficultyModerate
EquipmentSmoker or oven; sheet pan; skillet; thermometer.
Make-ahead: Mix and shape the loaf up to 1 day ahead. Gravy can be made 2 days ahead and gently reheated.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 lb ground pork
  • 4 slices bacon, finely chopped
  • 1/2 small yellow onion, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup crushed buttery crackers
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 3/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • For gravy: 1 tbsp butter, 1 large onion thinly sliced, 1 tbsp flour, 1/2 cup amber lager, 1 cup beef broth, 1 tsp Worcestershire, 1/2 tsp Dijon, black pepper

Step-by-step

  1. Set smoker to 250°F or oven to 350°F.
  2. Cook the diced onion in butter 5–6 minutes until soft; cool slightly.
  3. Mix crackers and milk in a large bowl and let stand 3 minutes. Add beef, pork, bacon, cooked onion, egg, Worcestershire, Dijon, salt, pepper, onion powder and paprika. Mix gently.
  4. Shape into a compact free-form loaf about 8x4 inches on a lined sheet pan.
  5. Mix ketchup, brown sugar and Worcestershire. Brush half over the loaf.
  6. Smoke 60–80 minutes or bake 45–60 minutes. Brush with remaining glaze during the last 15 minutes. Cook to 160°F in the center.
  7. Meanwhile make gravy: melt butter and cook sliced onion over medium-low 15–20 minutes until deeply golden. Stir in flour for 1 minute.
  8. Whisk in beer slowly, then broth, Worcestershire, Dijon and pepper. Simmer 6–10 minutes until glossy and gravy-like.
  9. Rest meatloaf 10 minutes, slice thickly and spoon beer-onion gravy over each piece.
KaySo note: For cleaner slices, let the meatloaf rest. If the gravy tastes bitter from the beer, add 1/2 teaspoon brown sugar.
Supper Club Pork Chops with Brandy-Dijon Peppercorn CreamOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3 servings
Total Time45 minutes
DifficultyModerate
EquipmentHeavy skillet; thermometer.
Make-ahead: Season chops a few hours ahead. Sauce should be finished just before serving.

Ingredients

  • 3 thick-cut boneless or bone-in pork chops, 1–1 1/4 inches thick
  • 1 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp coarse black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 small shallot, minced
  • 1/4 cup brandy
  • 3/4 cup chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp coarsely cracked black pepper

Step-by-step

  1. Pat chops dry and season with salt, pepper and garlic powder. Let stand at room temperature 15–20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high. Sear chops 3–4 minutes on the first side and 2–3 minutes on the second.
  3. Reduce heat to medium. Add butter and baste briefly. Continue cooking until chops reach 138–140°F. Transfer to a plate and tent loosely.
  4. Lower heat. Add shallot to the same skillet and cook 45 seconds.
  5. Carefully pour in brandy and let it bubble 1–2 minutes, scraping up browned bits.
  6. Add broth and simmer until reduced by about one-third, 3–4 minutes.
  7. Stir in cream, Dijon, Worcestershire and cracked pepper. Simmer gently 3–5 minutes until the sauce coats a spoon.
  8. Return the chops and any juices to the skillet for 1 minute. Serve at 145°F with sauce spooned generously over the top.
KaySo note: Do not boil cream sauce aggressively after adding the cream. Gentle bubbling keeps it smooth.
Smoked Stuffed Flank Steak PinwheelsOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3–4 servings
Total Time1 hour 35 minutes
DifficultyAdvanced
EquipmentSmoker; butcher’s twine; sharp slicing knife; thermometer.
Make-ahead: Make the caramelized onions and bacon 1 day ahead. Roll and tie steak up to 6 hours ahead.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb flank steak
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 3/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 4 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley or chives, optional
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil

Step-by-step

  1. Set smoker to 250°F. Cook onion in butter with brown sugar and a pinch of salt over medium-low heat 20 minutes until caramelized. Cool.
  2. Lay flank steak with the grain running left to right. If very thick, carefully butterfly it from a long side. Pound lightly to an even 1/2-inch thickness.
  3. Season inside with half the salt and pepper and spread with Dijon.
  4. Layer caramelized onions, bacon, cheddar and herbs, leaving a 1-inch border.
  5. Roll tightly so the finished cylinder runs across the grain; this ensures the slices themselves are tender when cut.
  6. Tie securely every 1 1/2 inches. Rub outside with oil and remaining seasoning.
  7. Smoke until the center reaches about 120–125°F, usually 45–60 minutes.
  8. Sear over very high direct heat or in a cast-iron skillet 60–90 seconds per side until well browned. Pull around 130–135°F for medium-rare to medium.
  9. Rest 10–15 minutes. Remove twine and slice into 3/4-inch pinwheels.
KaySo note: Flank steak can become chewy if sliced with the grain. The roll orientation matters; when you slice the finished log into rounds, you want the visible steak fibers cut short.
Smoked Bourbon-BBQ Pork Chops with Bacon-Onion JamOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3 servings
Total Time1 hour 15 minutes
DifficultyModerate
EquipmentSmoker or grill; skillet; thermometer.
Make-ahead: Bacon-onion jam can be made 3 days ahead. Chops can be seasoned the morning of.

Ingredients

  • 3 thick-cut pork chops
  • 1 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/3 cup BBQ sauce
  • 2 tbsp bourbon
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • For jam: 5 slices bacon chopped, 1 large onion diced, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar, 1 tbsp bourbon, 1/2 tsp Dijon

Step-by-step

  1. Set smoker to 250°F. Season chops with salt, paprika, pepper and onion powder.
  2. Smoke chops until they reach 125–130°F, roughly 30–45 minutes depending on thickness.
  3. Meanwhile make the jam: cook bacon in a skillet until crisp. Remove about half for later, leaving 1–2 tablespoons fat.
  4. Add onion and cook over medium-low 12–15 minutes until very soft and browned. Stir in brown sugar, vinegar, bourbon and Dijon. Cook 3–4 minutes until sticky, then fold the reserved bacon back in.
  5. Mix BBQ sauce, bourbon, brown sugar, Dijon and Worcestershire in a small saucepan. Simmer 3 minutes.
  6. Increase grill heat to high or heat a cast-iron skillet. Sear the smoked chops 1–2 minutes per side.
  7. Brush with bourbon-BBQ glaze during the final minute, turning carefully so it caramelizes without burning.
  8. Pull at 140–142°F and rest 5 minutes. Spoon bacon-onion jam over each chop.
KaySo note: The jam should be savory first, sweet second. If it tastes too sweet, add another teaspoon of vinegar.
