Loaded Bacon-Cheddar Potato SaladOpen Recipe ↓
Serves4 side servings
Total Time45 minutes + chilling
DifficultyEasy
EquipmentLarge saucepan; mixing bowl.
Make-ahead: Best made 4–24 hours ahead.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into 3/4-inch chunks
  • 5 slices bacon, cooked crisp and chopped
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 3/4 cup sharp cheddar, small cubes or shredded
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/3 cup sour cream
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Dijon or yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Step-by-step

  1. Put potatoes in a saucepan and cover with cold water by 1 inch. Salt the water generously.
  2. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer and cook 9–12 minutes until a knife slides in easily but the potatoes still hold their shape.
  3. Drain well and spread on a tray for 10 minutes so steam escapes.
  4. Whisk mayo, sour cream, mustard, vinegar, paprika, salt and pepper in a large bowl.
  5. Add warm—not hot—potatoes and fold gently to coat.
  6. Once cooled to room temperature, fold in bacon, eggs, cheddar and scallions.
  7. Cover and chill at least 2 hours. Before serving, taste again; cold potatoes often need another pinch of salt or splash of vinegar.
KaySo note: Reserve a little bacon, cheddar and scallion for the top so it looks intentional instead of like beige potato salad.
Wisconsin Supper Club Potato SaladOpen Recipe ↓
Serves4 side servings
Total Time45 minutes + chilling
DifficultyEasy
EquipmentLarge saucepan; bowl.
Make-ahead: Best made the day before.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb red potatoes, cut into bite-size chunks
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 1/3 cup finely diced celery
  • 1/4 cup finely diced red or yellow onion
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sour cream
  • 1 1/2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp paprika, plus more for garnish

Step-by-step

  1. Cover potatoes with cold salted water. Bring to a boil and simmer 9–12 minutes until just tender.
  2. Drain and let steam-dry 10 minutes.
  3. Whisk mayo, sour cream, mustard, vinegar, sugar, salt, pepper and paprika.
  4. Fold the slightly warm potatoes into the dressing so they absorb some flavor.
  5. When cool, fold in egg, celery and onion.
  6. Cover and chill at least 3 hours, preferably overnight.
  7. Before serving, loosen with 1 tablespoon milk or sour cream if needed, then dust lightly with paprika.
KaySo note: This one is intentionally classic: savory, mustardy and creamy, without pickles or sweet relish.
Supper Club Twice-Baked PotatoesOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3 large or 6 half-potato servings
Total Time1 hour 35 minutes
DifficultyEasy–Moderate
EquipmentSheet pan; mixing bowl.
Make-ahead: Fully assemble up to 1 day ahead; bake the second time just before serving.

Ingredients

  • 3 large russet potatoes
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, divided
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1/3 cup sour cream
  • 1/4 cup whole milk
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar, divided
  • 4 slices bacon, crisp and chopped
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Step-by-step

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Scrub potatoes, dry, rub with oil and sprinkle lightly with salt.
  2. Bake directly on the rack or on a sheet pan for 50–65 minutes until very tender.
  3. Cool 10 minutes, then slice lengthwise. Scoop the centers into a bowl, leaving about 1/4 inch potato attached to the skins.
  4. Mash the hot potato with butter, sour cream, milk, 2/3 cup cheddar, most of the bacon, most of the scallions, salt and pepper.
  5. Spoon filling generously back into the shells. Top with remaining cheddar and bacon.
  6. Return to 400°F oven for 15–20 minutes until hot through and browned at the edges.
  7. Finish with remaining scallions.
KaySo note: For game-day timing, complete through filling the shells earlier in the day and refrigerate. Add 5–10 minutes to the final bake if cold.
Wisconsin Italian Pasta SaladOpen Recipe ↓
Serves4–5 side servings
Total Time30 minutes + chilling
DifficultyEasy
EquipmentLarge pot; colander; mixing bowl.
Make-ahead: Best 4–24 hours ahead. Reserve a little dressing to refresh before serving.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz rotini
  • 3 oz salami, diced
  • 2 oz pepperoni, quartered
  • 3 oz mozzarella, cubed
  • 3 oz sharp cheddar, cubed
  • 1/4 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/3 cup sliced pepperoncini
  • 1/2 cup Italian dressing, plus 2 tbsp for refreshing
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Step-by-step

  1. Cook rotini in well-salted water until just al dente. Do not overcook.
  2. Drain and rinse briefly under cool water just until the pasta stops steaming. Drain extremely well.
  3. Toss pasta with 1/2 cup dressing and black pepper.
  4. Fold in salami, pepperoni, mozzarella, cheddar, red onion, pepperoncini and Parmesan.
  5. Cover and chill at least 2 hours.
  6. Before serving, toss with the reserved 2 tablespoons dressing. Taste and add salt only if needed; the meats and cheese are already salty.
KaySo note: For a less sharp onion flavor, soak sliced red onion in cold water for 10 minutes, then dry it well before adding.
Smoked Wisconsin Tavern Pizza SquaresOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3–4 as a side/snack
Total Time45 minutes
DifficultyModerate
EquipmentSmoker capable of 425–500°F; 12-inch pizza pan or cast iron; rolling pin.
Make-ahead: Cook sausage and prep toppings earlier. Dough can be stretched onto the pan 30 minutes before cooking.

Ingredients

  • 8–10 oz pizza dough
  • 1/3 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella
  • 1/3 cup shredded sharp cheddar
  • 3 oz Italian sausage, cooked and crumbled
  • 12–15 pepperoni slices
  • 1/4 small onion, very thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Pinch dried oregano
  • Pinch crushed red pepper, optional

Step-by-step

  1. Heat smoker to 450°F if possible. Put your pizza pan or cast-iron pan inside for 10 minutes to preheat.
  2. Roll dough very thin—thinner than a normal pizza—into roughly a 12-inch round or rectangle.
  3. Carefully oil the hot pan and lay the dough onto it. Prick the center several times with a fork.
  4. Par-cook the crust 3–4 minutes until the surface looks dry and the bottom has begun to set.
  5. Spread a thin layer of sauce. Add mozzarella, cheddar, sausage, pepperoni and onion. Keep toppings restrained so the crust stays crisp.
  6. Return to smoker 8–12 minutes until the bottom is crisp and cheese is browned in spots.
  7. Rest 3–5 minutes, sprinkle with oregano, then cut into small tavern-style squares.
KaySo note: If your smoker will not exceed 375°F, smoke the assembled pizza 5 minutes for flavor and finish it in a 475°F oven.
