Hot Ham, Swiss & Cheddar Sliders
⏱ 30 minutesVery Easy
Open recipe
Serves3–4 late-night servings • 9 sliders
Total time30 minutes
DifficultyVery Easy
Equipment8x8 or 9x9 baking dish; foil.
Make-ahead: Assemble before leaving for Lambeau; refrigerate covered. Add topping and bake when you get home.

Ingredients

  • 9 connected Hawaiian or soft dinner rolls
  • 8 oz thin-sliced ham
  • 4 slices Swiss cheese
  • 3/4 cup shredded sharp cheddar
  • 3 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp poppy seeds, optional

Step-by-step

  1. Slice the entire slab of rolls horizontally without separating individual rolls.
  2. Place bottom slab in a lightly greased baking dish.
  3. Layer ham evenly, then Swiss and cheddar. Replace the top slab.
  4. If making ahead, cover and refrigerate at this point.
  5. When ready to bake, heat oven to 350°F.
  6. Mix melted butter, Dijon, Worcestershire, onion powder and poppy seeds. Brush over the tops.
  7. Cover loosely with foil and bake 12 minutes. Uncover and bake 5–8 minutes more until tops are browned and cheese is fully melted.
  8. Cut into individual sliders and serve hot.
KaySo note: These are intentionally simple after-game food. Do the assembly before you leave so late-night cooking is basically just preheat, brush and bake.
Postgame Tavern Pizza
⏱ 15–25 minutes after you get homeEasy
Open recipe
Serves3–4
Total time15–25 minutes after you get home
DifficultyEasy
EquipmentPizza pan or cast iron; oven or smoker.
Make-ahead: This recipe is designed around prep-before-the-game.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz pizza dough
  • 1/3 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/4 cup sharp cheddar
  • 3 oz cooked Italian sausage
  • 15 pepperoni slices
  • 1/4 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp oil

Step-by-step

  1. Before leaving: cook the sausage, slice the onion and portion all toppings.
  2. For the fastest postgame version, roll the dough thin, place it on an oiled pan and par-bake at 475°F for 4–5 minutes. Cool completely.
  3. Top the cooled par-baked crust with sauce, cheeses, sausage, pepperoni and onion.
  4. Cover the entire pan tightly and refrigerate while you are at the game.
  5. When you get home, heat oven to 475°F.
  6. Uncover and bake the cold prepared pizza 8–12 minutes until the cheese is browned and the bottom is crisp.
  7. Rest 3 minutes and cut into tavern squares.
KaySo note: If tavern pizza also wins another round, you can prep two crusts at once. If it doesn’t, this recipe still works completely independently.
Hot Roast Beef & Cheddar Sliders + Au Jus
⏱ 30 minutesVery Easy
Open recipe
Serves3–4 • 9 sliders
Total time30 minutes
DifficultyVery Easy
Equipment8x8 or 9x9 baking dish; small saucepan.
Make-ahead: Assemble sliders and mix au-jus ingredients before leaving.

Ingredients

  • 9 soft slider rolls
  • 10 oz thinly sliced deli roast beef
  • 1 1/4 cups shredded sharp cheddar
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 tsp Dijon
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • For au jus: 1 1/2 cups beef broth, 1 tsp Worcestershire, 1/2 tsp onion powder, 1/4 tsp black pepper, 1 tsp butter

Step-by-step

  1. Slice connected rolls horizontally and place bottoms in a greased baking dish.
  2. Layer roast beef and cheddar evenly. Replace tops. Cover and refrigerate until after the game.
  3. When ready, heat oven to 350°F.
  4. Mix melted butter, Dijon, Worcestershire and onion powder. Brush over roll tops.
  5. Cover with foil and bake 12 minutes, then uncover and bake 5–8 minutes more.
  6. While sliders bake, simmer beef broth, Worcestershire, onion powder and pepper for 5 minutes. Whisk in butter at the end.
  7. Cut sliders apart and serve with small cups of hot au jus.
KaySo note: Use deli roast beef sliced thin rather than thick steak slices; it stays tender during the second heating.
Loaded Pizza Fries
⏱ 30 minutesEasy
Open recipe
Serves3 hungry late-night snackers
Total time30 minutes
DifficultyEasy
EquipmentLarge sheet pan; oven or air fryer for fries.
Make-ahead: Cook sausage and portion toppings before the game.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb frozen crispy fries
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 2 oz pepperoni, chopped
  • 4 oz Italian sausage, cooked and crumbled
  • 1/4 cup shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 cup pizza sauce, warmed
  • Crushed red pepper or Parmesan, optional

Step-by-step

  1. Cook fries according to package directions, but take them 2–3 minutes past merely done; they need to be very crisp before toppings go on.
  2. Push fries into a fairly tight single layer on the pan.
  3. Scatter mozzarella, pepperoni, sausage and cheddar evenly over the fries.
  4. Return to a 425°F oven for 4–7 minutes until cheese is melted and starting to brown.
  5. Sprinkle with Italian seasoning and optional Parmesan or red pepper.
  6. Serve immediately with warm pizza sauce on the side for dipping instead of pouring sauce over the fries and making them soggy.
KaySo note: Crisp fries first, toppings second. That one decision is the difference between pizza fries and a tray of wet potatoes.
Meatloaf Melts
⏱ 20 minutesEasy
Open recipe
Serves3 sandwiches
Total time20 minutes
DifficultyEasy
EquipmentSkillet or griddle.
Make-ahead: Conditional on Tavern Meatloaf winning. Slice meatloaf and onions before the game.

Ingredients

  • 3 thick slices leftover Wisconsin Tavern Meatloaf
  • 6 slices sturdy sourdough, rye or Texas toast
  • 3 slices Swiss cheese
  • 3 slices sharp cheddar
  • 1/2 cup caramelized onions
  • 3 tbsp softened butter
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mayo: 1 tbsp mayo + 1 tbsp Dijon
  • Optional warm leftover beer-onion gravy for dipping

Step-by-step

  1. Warm meatloaf slices gently in a skillet over medium-low heat or microwave just until hot.
  2. Spread Dijon mayo on one side of each bread slice.
  3. Build each sandwich with Swiss, warm meatloaf, caramelized onions and cheddar. Close sandwiches with spread sides facing inward.
  4. Butter the outsides of the bread.
  5. Cook on a griddle over medium-low heat 3–5 minutes per side, pressing gently, until deeply golden and both cheeses are melted.
  6. Rest 2 minutes before cutting.
  7. Serve with warm leftover beer-onion gravy for dipping if you have it.
KaySo note: Medium-low heat matters. High heat burns the bread before thick meatloaf and two cheeses warm through.
