Smoked Tavern Pizza SquaresOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3–4 snack servings
Total Time45 minutes
DifficultyModerate
EquipmentSmoker; pizza pan or cast iron.
Make-ahead: Same pizza can be prepped earlier and cooked shortly before leaving.

Ingredients

  • 8–10 oz pizza dough
  • 1/3 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 1/3 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 2 oz pepperoni
  • 3 oz cooked Italian sausage
  • 1/4 onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp sharp cheddar
  • 1 tsp olive oil

Step-by-step

  1. Heat smoker to 450°F and preheat the pan.
  2. Roll dough very thin and par-cook 3–4 minutes on the hot pan.
  3. Top lightly with sauce, cheeses, pepperoni, sausage and onion.
  4. Smoke/bake 8–12 minutes until crisp underneath and browned on top.
  5. Rest briefly and cut into small squares so it stays a snack rather than turning into another full meal.
KaySo note: This is deliberately the same food concept as the Halftime pizza. It lives in one slot or the other on the final menu—not both unless you genuinely want a lot of pizza.
Pizza Roll-UpsOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3 people • 12 roll-ups
Total Time35 minutes
DifficultyEasy
EquipmentSheet pan; parchment.
Make-ahead: Roll, slice and refrigerate up to 6 hours before baking.

Ingredients

  • 1 tube refrigerated pizza dough or 10 oz homemade dough
  • 1/3 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 1/4 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 24 small pepperoni slices
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan
  • 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Warm pizza sauce for dipping

Step-by-step

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Line a pan with parchment.
  2. Roll dough into roughly a 10x14-inch rectangle.
  3. Spread with a very thin layer of sauce, leaving a 1/2-inch border. Add mozzarella, pepperoni, Parmesan and Italian seasoning.
  4. Roll tightly from a long side into a log.
  5. Use a sharp knife to cut 12 equal rounds.
  6. Place cut-side up with a little space between them. Bake 16–20 minutes until deeply golden and cooked through in the centers.
  7. Cool 5 minutes and serve with warm pizza sauce.
KaySo note: Too much sauce inside makes these soggy and difficult to roll. Keep the internal sauce thin and use extra for dipping.
Bacon-Cheddar Ranch PinwheelsOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3 people • 18 pinwheels
Total Time20 minutes + chilling
DifficultyVery Easy
EquipmentMixing bowl; plastic wrap; sharp knife.
Make-ahead: Excellent 1 day ahead.

Ingredients

  • 2 large burrito-size flour tortillas
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tbsp sour cream
  • 1 tsp ranch seasoning
  • 3/4 cup finely shredded sharp cheddar
  • 4 slices bacon, crisp and finely chopped
  • 2 scallions, very thinly sliced
  • Black pepper

Step-by-step

  1. Mix cream cheese, sour cream and ranch seasoning until smooth.
  2. Fold in cheddar, bacon, scallions and a few grinds of black pepper.
  3. Spread half the mixture edge-to-edge over each tortilla.
  4. Roll each tortilla as tightly as possible into a log.
  5. Wrap firmly in plastic and chill at least 1 hour.
  6. Trim the ragged ends, then slice each log into about 9 pinwheels with a sharp serrated knife.
  7. Keep cold until serving.
KaySo note: Do not overdo the ranch seasoning; the bacon and cheddar already bring plenty of salt.
Wisconsin Party Rye MeltsOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3 people • 12 rye melts
Total Time30 minutes
DifficultyEasy
EquipmentSheet pan; skillet.
Make-ahead: Make the topping 1 day ahead. Assemble just before baking.

Ingredients

  • 12 slices cocktail rye bread
  • 6 oz breakfast sausage or mild Italian sausage
  • 3/4 cup shredded sharp cheddar
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon or yellow mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp very finely minced onion
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Step-by-step

  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Brown sausage in a skillet, breaking it into very small crumbles. Drain well and cool 5 minutes.
  3. Mix sausage with cheddar, mayo, mustard, Worcestershire, onion and pepper.
  4. Arrange cocktail rye on a sheet pan. Spoon about 1 tablespoon topping onto each slice and spread close to the edges.
  5. Bake 8–10 minutes until the bread edges crisp and the topping is hot and melted.
  6. Broil 30–60 seconds if you want a browned top, watching closely.
  7. Serve immediately.
KaySo note: This is old-school party food. Cocktail rye is part of the charm, but small slices of regular rye work if that’s what you can find.
Baked Bacon-Cheddar Sausage BallsOpen Recipe ↓
Serves3 people • about 18 balls
Total Time35 minutes
DifficultyEasy
EquipmentSheet pan; parchment.
Make-ahead: Shape and refrigerate up to 1 day ahead, or freeze raw balls and bake from frozen with extra time.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz breakfast sausage
  • 1 cup biscuit baking mix
  • 1 cup finely shredded sharp cheddar
  • 3 slices bacon, cooked and finely chopped
  • 2 scallions, minced
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • Black pepper
  • Mustard or ranch for serving

Step-by-step

  1. Heat oven to 400°F and line a sheet pan.
  2. Combine sausage, biscuit mix, cheddar, bacon, scallions, milk, paprika and black pepper.
  3. Use your hands to knead gently until no dry pockets remain. If the mixture refuses to hold together, add 1 more tablespoon milk.
  4. Roll into 18 balls, about 1 inch each.
  5. Bake 17–21 minutes, turning the pan halfway through, until browned and the sausage reaches 160°F.
  6. Rest 5 minutes before serving with mustard or ranch.
KaySo note: Finely shredded cheese works better than thick shreds because it integrates into the dough instead of leaking out in giant puddles.
